## How to prepare further questions

Read the relevant original page and the master syllabus first. Use short instructions and familiar words. Offer a balanced mix of short answers, true/false, fill-in-the-blanks, multiple choice, labeling/drawing, and simple application questions. Keep most model answers to one or two sentences; use a few short points when a list is required. Preserve Bangla for Bangla exercises.

Attach a real document ID and PDF page number to every question. For images such as textbook page 95, the local document has one page: cite page 1 and retain 95 in the document title. The unseen pages supplied are 95–102 only; do not claim earlier pages are present.

For mathematics, vary numbers within the level actually present in the school notes: four-digit place value, comparisons, addition, subtraction, and basic geometry. The syllabus explicitly says numerical values will change. Show carrying/borrowing when asked.

Separate a question prompt from an answer source. Unfilled assignments are not answer keys. Use a corresponding study note when available and cite both. Do not produce unsupported answers to a story or diagram that was not uploaded. Mark invented examples as practice examples.

## Scope gaps and source quality

The full Dumbo story, Emperor Ashoka chapter, several geography textbook chapters, Harrap spelling sets, and separate Mental Mathematics exercise pages are not attached. Some are listed only in the syllabus. The geography PDF contains the UK countries/capitals map only.

PDF text extraction was used where readable. Scans and legacy-font Bangla required OCR. Original page images are authoritative for unclear words, handwriting, diagrams and যুক্তবর্ণ. A blank second routine page is preserved.

Known source issues: the Joy of Math sheet calls 1 the smallest one-digit number (clarify positive counting numbers versus 0); an early EL2 example says feet → feet but its answer key correctly says feet → foot. Avoid turning simplified historical superlatives or appearance-based community descriptions into general factual assertions. Model answers are study aids, not an official school answer key.
